# Design Choices for Chai Culture

## 1. Visual Language & Color Palette
To capture the essence of "Royal Tradition," the design moves away from modern minimalism (stark whites) and embraces a warm, heritage-inspired palette.

### Global Theme
- **Background**: **Warm Cream (#F5F0E6)** overlaid with a `radial-gradient` and a subtle mandala wallpaper (`opacity: 0.5`) for texture.
- **Primary Text**: **Deep Brown (#2C241B)** representing the strength of tea and earthiness.
- **Accent Gold**: **Deep Copper Gold (#A65210)** for strong contrast on light backgrounds.

### About Section (Dark Theme)
- **Background**: **Rich Cocoa (#5e321b)** to create a distinct, immersive storytelling section.
- **Text**: **Pale Cream (#EDE8D0)** for excellent readability against the dark background.
- **Highlight**: **Bright Gold (#EABE53)** for key phrases like "Sipped like royalty."

## 2. Typography Strategy
The typography was updated to enhance the "Editorial" and "Indian Heritage" feel.

- **Primary Heading (Serif)**: **`Cormorant Garamond`**. chosen for its elegant, flowing curves that mimic traditional calligraphy. It feels more organic and historical than standard serifs.
- **Body Text (Sans-Serif)**: **`Montserrat`**. A geometric yet humanist sans-serif. It provides a clean, modern counterpoint to the decorative headings, ensuring high readability on all devices.

## 3. Interaction Design
The interactions are intentionally subtle to maintain composure and confidence ("whispering luxury"). Instead of aggressive pop-ups, the "Launching Soon" indicator uses a gentle pulse animation, representing a heartbeat or a brewing process. The hover effects on buttons and social icons are smooth and restrained, reinforcing the brand's calm and sophisticated identity.

## 4. Mobile Optimization & Recent Updates
### Layout Adjustments
- **Hero Image**: Hidden on mobile devices (` < 1024px`) to prevent clutter and keep focus on the strong brand typography.
- **Signup Form**: Constrained to `350px` width on mobile for a centered, clean input field, while expanding to full width on desktop.

### Assets
- **Hero Image**: Renamed to `chai-cup.png`. Positioned lower (`translateY(120px)`) to balance the composition.
- **Background Image**: Visibility significantly increased (Opacity 0.5) to make the royal patterns stand out.
